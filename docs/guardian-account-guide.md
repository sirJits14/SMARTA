# Guardian account guide — create & verify your account

For parents/guardians using the BNHS Learner Records parent portal
(`https://bnhs-parent.web.app`). Matches the flow in `parent/src/screens/`
and the copy in `parent/src/strings.js` — update both together if either
changes.

## Before you start

You need an **activation slip** from the school (printed through the
registrar's normal pipeline, handed out in homeroom). It has an 8-character
activation code. No slip yet? Skip to [No activation slip?](#no-activation-slip)
below.

## Steps

1. **Open the portal.** Go to `https://bnhs-parent.web.app` on your phone or
   computer. On iPhone/iPad, for notifications to work later, add it to your
   Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

2. **Create your account.**
   - Tap **Sign in with Google** to use an existing Google account (already
     verified — skip to step 4), **or**
   - Tap **Sign in with email**, then **Create an account**. Enter your
     email and a password of at least 8 characters.

3. **Verify your email** (email/password accounts only). Check your inbox
   for the verification message and tap the link in it. Back in the portal,
   tap **Continue**. If nothing arrived, use **Send the email again** — it
   can take a minute, and check spam.

4. **Read and accept the privacy notice.** The portal explains what it
   records (your name, email, relationship to the learner, notification
   devices) and how long gate-scan records are kept. Check the box and
   continue.

5. **Link your learner.** Enter the 8-character **activation code** from
   your slip and your **relationship to the learner** (Mother, Father,
   Guardian, Grandparent, Sibling, Other), then tap **Link learner**.

6. **Done.** You'll land on Home showing today's gate scans for your
   learner. From **Settings** you can turn on notifications for this
   device, view history, report a record you think is wrong, or delete
   your account.

## No activation slip?

On the activation screen, tap **No slip? Ask the school for access** and
submit the learner's LRN, full name, and your contact number. The registrar
reviews the request; you'll see its status (waiting / approved / not
approved) under Settings → My access requests.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Forgot your password | On the email sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and check your inbox. |
| Verification email never arrives | Tap **Send the email again** on the Verify screen; check spam; confirm the email address was typed correctly. |
| Activation code doesn't work | Codes are single-use and expire. Ask the registrar to reissue your slip. |
| Notifications won't turn on | Check your browser's site permissions allow Notifications for this page; on iPhone/iPad, the page must be opened from the Home Screen icon, not Safari directly. |
| Need to remove a learner | Guardians can't unlink themselves — ask the school registrar. |
