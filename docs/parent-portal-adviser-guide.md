# Activation slips — adviser one-pager

1. The registrar gives you one slip per learner in your section.
2. Hand each slip **to the parent or guardian in person** (enrollment day,
   homeroom meeting, or card claiming). Do not send slips through learners
   or group chats — the slip is what proves the parent is entitled.
3. Tell the parent: open the portal on the slip (or tap **Scan the QR code
   on the slip** inside the app), sign in with Google or an email, agree to
   the notice; done. Up to two parents/guardians can use one slip. The slip
   works for the whole school year until the school ends it.
4. Lost slip, separated parents, custody orders, or "the app says the code
   can't be used": send the parent to the registrar, or have them tap
   "No slip? Ask the school for access" in the portal.
5. Parents receive a short notification when the learner scans at the gate.
   They see details only inside the app. The Inbox in the app is the
   complete record; a phone may miss a notification.

6. **Class advisers no longer link through learners' activation slips.**
   Slips are for parents and guardians only. To become a class adviser, see
   *Becoming a class adviser* below.

## Becoming a class adviser

1. Sign in to the parent portal with your **@deped.gov.ph** Google account
   and agree to the notice.
2. On **Who are you?**, enter your name and tick **Class Adviser**. You can
   also tick **Parent/Guardian** if you have a learner to link.
3. Ask the Administrator, or the JHS/SHS Academic Coordinator or Grade Level
   Coordinator for your grade, for your section's advisory slip. Scan its QR
   code or type its code.
4. Check the section name on the confirm screen, then confirm. You become the
   adviser at once; no approval is needed. The slip works once.
5. **No slip yet?** When the school has opened section requests, request
   your section instead, with your grade and section. An Administrator or
   your grade's Curriculum Adviser approves it, and the request shows as
   *Waiting for approval* until then.
6. Already using the app? Open **Settings → Add a role**.

**No DepEd email?** The Class Adviser option appears only for
**@deped.gov.ph** accounts. An Administrator can still make any account a
class adviser under **School access** in SIMS.

## Downloading the SF2

1. Open **Class** in the parent app and tap **Download SF2**.
2. Choose the month. The list shows this school year's months up to the current one.
3. Tap **Download**. On a phone the share sheet opens: choose **Save to Files**, **Drive**, or open the file in Excel. On a computer the file downloads.
4. The file is the same SF2 that SIMS exports: your learners (boys first, then girls), the daily marks, the totals and your name. Check it before you print or submit it.

If the button is greyed out, your class has no learners yet. If you see "Too many exports", wait a few minutes and try again.
